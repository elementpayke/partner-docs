# International deposit accounts

Open **bank deposit coordinates** and optional **stablecoin wallets** for your end-customers after entity KYC, under public Partner routes `/partner/entities/*`. Then show deposit instructions and move money between accounts under your API key.

This is a **separate product** from:

- African local-fiat **quote to accept** ([Quickstart](/quickstart), [Corridors](/corridors/overview))
- Catalog **`international_bank`** EUR/USD payin via quote to accept (a one-shot ramp, not reusable deposit accounts)

Use **one product per checkout**; do not mix entity deposit accounts with a local OnRamp/OffRamp quote in the same flow.

## Capability matrix

Honest **Ready / Limited / Hold** for integrators (same shapes as [errors](/errors) and account statuses). Prefer the column status over marketing language.

| Job | Status | Notes |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Individual KYC | **Ready** | Wait for `approved` |
| Business KYB | **Ready** | Prefer single-officer path first; multi-officer has PoA / document constraints. See [Entity KYC](/deposit-accounts/kyc). |
| Open EUR / GBP / CAD (fiat) | **Ready** | When `active` + deposit-instructions |
| Open USDC / USDT wallets | **Ready** | When `active`; address returned. On-chain inflows usually credit above a small floor (see [Fund an account](/deposit-accounts/accounts#fund-an-account)); still wait for balance / `account.credited` |
| Open USD (business) | **Ready** (most) / **Limited** (e.g. KE business unconfirmed) | Often reaches `active`. Poll until active for deposit-instructions or [card issuing](/deposit-accounts/card-issuing). If KE (or other) business USD stays `pending`, contact Element Pay admin |
| Open USD (individual) | **Limited** | KE individuals: **USD bank not available** (other fiats OK). Other individuals may lag bank-details KYC after entity `approved` (not always “slow provisioning” only) |
| Fiat book-transfer | **Ready** | Same currency, accounts under your API key |
| Stablecoin or inter-entity “book-transfer” | **Hold** | Product does **not** exist. Fiat book-transfer only. |
| External USDC / USDT send | **Ready** (live) | Prefer external wallets for GA; sandbox confirm **limited** |
| Send → another party’s standing deposit wallet | **Limited** | Prefer external wallets; standing-wallet dest not GA |
| Convert USDT↔CAD/EUR, USDT→USD, USD→USDC, EUR→USDC | **Ready** | Same entity; **active** accounts; check quote `limits` (often ≥1 crypto unit) |
| GBP OffRamp convert | **Hold** | Until live accept settles; quote `crypto_amount` often ≥ `1` (see [Currency conversion](/deposit-accounts/currency-conversion)) |
| Card **issue** (virtual / disposable on active USD) | **Ready** | Live on active USD; sandbox e2e may be limited |
| Card freeze / unfreeze / list / get / transactions | **API Ready** | Transactions empty until real spend |
| Card **funding** (card-charges into fiat) | **Limited** | USD: any billing/card country. GBP/EUR: **UK + EU only**. Settlement lag. See [Card funding](/deposit-accounts/card-funding). |

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Entity KYC">
    Create an individual or business, upload documents, submit, and wait for `approved`. See [Entity KYC](/deposit-accounts/kyc).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open accounts">
    Open fiat (EUR / GBP / USD / CAD as enabled) and/or stablecoin (USDC / USDT) rails, then wait until `active` and fetch deposit instructions. See [Accounts & deposits](/deposit-accounts/accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund and move money">
    Fund via deposit instructions (and [card funding](/deposit-accounts/card-funding) where Limited is acceptable). Issue cards on **active USD** ([Card issuing](/deposit-accounts/card-issuing)). [Convert](/deposit-accounts/currency-conversion) on-entity, [book-transfer](/deposit-accounts/book-transfers) fiat, or [send](/deposit-accounts/sends) stablecoin to an external wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When to use this

| Use this when… | Prefer another product when… |
|----------------|------------------------------|
| Your customer needs reusable **deposit instructions** (bank fields and/or an on-chain wallet) after KYC | They pay **local African fiat** (momo/bank) for a one-shot ramp, see [Quickstart](/quickstart) |
| You key customers by your own `customer_ref` and open rails after `approved` | You only need a binding FX quote for a single order |

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Entity** | Your end-customer (individual or business), scoped to your API key, keyed by **`customer_ref`** |
| **Entity status** | `pending` → `submitted` → `approved` \| `rejected` \| `suspended` |
| **Account** | A deposit rail on an **approved** entity (fiat and/or stablecoin) |
| **Fiat account** | e.g. EUR / GBP / USD / CAD when enabled; bank fields when `active` |
| **Stablecoin account** | e.g. USDC or USDT on a stated `network`, with `wallet_address` when ready |
| **Balance** | `balance: { available, current, currency }` when known (may be `null` if refresh fails) |
| **Partner account status** | `pending` until coordinates are usable, then `active` (also `failed` \| `closed`) |

<Note>
Stablecoin rails (e.g. USDC) are **independent ledgers**. They are **not** tied to a USD fiat account balance pot. Open fiat USD and USDC independently as needed.
</Note>

Partners **cannot** register their own wallet as the deposit address. Custody stays on the deposit rail Element Pay provisions.

**Idempotency:** entity create is unique per API key + `customer_ref`. Account open is unique per `(entity, asset_type, currency, network)` (`network` empty for fiat): re-open returns the existing account (`200`); first open returns `201`.

## Status vocabulary

| Object | States |
|--------|--------|
| **Entity** | `pending` → `submitted` → `approved` \| `rejected` \| `suspended` |
| **Account** | `pending` (no usable coords) → `active` (coords usable); also `failed` \| `closed` |
| **Book-transfer** | `pending` \| `completed` \| `failed` (as returned by confirm) |
| **Currency conversion** (order) | `processing` → `settled` \| `failed` |
| **Send** | `processing` → `submitted` → `completed` \| `failed` |

### USD readiness

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `pending` | Open accepted; **no** usable bank coords yet (`account_number` may be null) |
| `active` | Coords present; deposit-instructions / convert / issue using that USD can proceed |

**Partner rules**

1. Open may return `201` while still `pending`.
2. Poll `GET …/accounts/{id}` until `status=active`, or wait for **`account.ready`**.
3. `GET …/deposit-instructions` while not ready → **422** `"Deposit instructions are not ready yet"` with `hint` to poll account or wait for `account.ready`.
4. Card issue on pending or non-USD → **422** readiness (active **USD** fiat only).
5. **KE individuals:** USD bank is **not available** (EUR/GBP/CAD and stablecoin rails still apply as enabled). Do not treat as temporary lag.
6. **Other individuals:** USD may stay `pending` after entity `approved` while bank-details KYC completes; not always “slow rail provisioning” alone.
7. **Business USD:** often reaches `active`. KE business USD is still unconfirmed; if it stays `pending`, contact Element Pay admin.

## Webhooks

Configure `webhook_url` (and `webhook_secret`) on your API key. See [Webhooks](/webhooks).

| Event | When |
|-------|------|
| `entity.status.changed` | KYC / KYB status changed |
| `account.opened` | Account open accepted |
| `account.ready` | Usable coordinates (deposit-ready) |
| `account.credited` | Inbound credit settled |
| `account.send.completed` / `account.send.failed` | External wallet send terminal |
| `card_charge.status.changed` | Card **funding** (acquiring) intent only, not card **issue** lifecycle |

**Issued cards** (virtual / disposable): no dedicated partner webhook for create / freeze / unfreeze today. Poll `GET …/cards/{id}` or list. Conversions emit **order** webhooks (`order.processing` / `order.settled` / `order.failed`).

## Errors

Same envelope as other Partner APIs ([Errors](/errors)):

```json
{
  "status": "error",
  "message": "Deposit instructions are not ready yet",
  "data": {
    "account_id": 17,
    "status": "pending",
    "hint": "Poll GET account or wait for account.ready"
  }
}
```

Do not surface raw upstream payloads to end-users.

## Sandbox notes

- Individuals often reach `approved` without liveness; businesses may stay `submitted` longer.
- KE individual **USD** is unavailable; other individual USD may stay `pending` after entity `approved`. Business USD often reaches `active` (confirm KE business separately).
- No free USDC / USDT grant in partner APIs. Sandbox balances may need ops seed; see [Fund an account](/deposit-accounts/accounts#fund-an-account).
- Stablecoin **send confirm** is **limited** in sandbox; **live** external sends are Ready.
